Caffeine In Olivaro: Guarana And Green Tea

The seller calls Olivaro "active" and "stimulating", and the label explains why.*

Two Caffeine Sources

Guarana seed extract and green tea leaf extract both contain caffeine. The label does not give the caffeine amount, so you cannot tell how much you are taking.
