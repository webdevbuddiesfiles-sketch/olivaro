Safety Checklist

When To See A Doctor, Not A Supplement

A supplement should never stand in for diabetes care.*

Call Your Clinician For

  • Readings far above or below your usual range
  • Heavy thirst, frequent urination or sudden weight loss
  • Repeated shaky, sweaty or confused spells
  • A racing heart, palpitations or new anxiety after a dose

See also: Blood sugar medicines · Caffeine and guarana

Seek Emergency Care For

  • Confusion, fainting or seizure
  • Chest pain
  • Trouble breathing, or swelling of the face or throat

See also: Caffeine and guarana · FAQ

Why It Matters

Blood sugar problems can become serious quickly and are managed with tests and medicines a supplement cannot replace.
