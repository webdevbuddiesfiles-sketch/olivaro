0.7 mcg, 2% DV

1. Chromium (picolinate)

A trace mineral involved in how the body handles carbohydrate. It is the one ingredient with its own amount on the label.

Studied for: Trials of chromium for blood sugar use roughly 200 to 1,000 mcg a day, with mixed results.

Worth knowing: At 0.7 mcg a serving, the label amount is a tiny fraction of those studied.

Guarana, green tea, Panax ginseng, eleuthero

2. Stimulating botanicals

Plants used for energy and alertness. Guarana and green tea carry caffeine; ginseng and eleuthero are adaptogens.

Studied for: Studied for energy, alertness and, in small trials, glucose measures.

Worth knowing: Caffeine amounts are not stated, and ginseng and green tea may interact with medicines or the liver.

Gymnema, African mango, raspberry ketones, coleus forskohlii, capsicum

3. Blood-sugar and weight botanicals

Plants and compounds marketed for sugar cravings, weight and metabolism.

Studied for: Small, mixed trials; gymnema has the most blood-sugar research.

Worth knowing: Several may add to diabetes medicines, and weight claims are weakly supported.

Maca, astragalus, grape seed, grapefruit extract

4. Adaptogens and antioxidants

Roots and extracts used for vitality, immunity and antioxidant support.

Studied for: Mostly traditional use and small trials.

Worth knowing: Grapefruit extract can change how some medicines are processed.

L-glutamine, L-tyrosine, L-arginine, beta-alanine, L-ornithine, L-tryptophan, L-carnitine, GABA

5. Amino acids and GABA

Protein building blocks and a calming messenger, sold for energy and recovery.

Studied for: Evidence depends on dose and use.

Worth knowing: Tryptophan can matter with antidepressants and tyrosine with thyroid medicine.

Monoammonium glycyrrhizinate

6. Licorice compound

A refined compound from licorice root, used as a sweet-tasting and soothing ingredient.

Studied for: Licorice-type compounds are known to affect blood pressure and potassium.

Worth knowing: Ask first with high blood pressure, heart medicine or diuretics.
