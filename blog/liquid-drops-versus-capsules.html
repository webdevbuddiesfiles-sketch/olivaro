Liquid Drops Versus Capsules

A dropper is easy, but it has trade-offs.

Why People Like Drops

No swallowing, and you can adjust the number of droppers. A liquid can also hold a blend that would not fit one capsule.

See also: Ingredients · Blood sugar medicines

Trade-Offs

Dropper volume varies, taste needs sweeteners such as xylitol and stevia, and preservatives are needed. Xylitol is dangerous to dogs.

Absorption claims for liquids are rarely proven. Compare the blend and habits that matter more, and ask your pharmacist.
